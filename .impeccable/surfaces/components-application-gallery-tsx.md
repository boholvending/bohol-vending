# Homepage application gallery

## Direction contract

THESIS: Replace the repeated insights teasers with three clearly disclosed application concepts. This is not evidence of customer deployments.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit BOHOL's Manrope/Space Grotesk typography, cool paper, ink and cyan, square image edges and restrained link feedback. Preserve the rest of the homepage and all article routes.

STORY: An overseas buyer sees the BOHOL cold-drink format in a lobby, shared lounge and office, understands the imagery is synthetic, and can discuss their own location through the existing inquiry section.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A stacked heading and short introduction precede a large left-hand landscape photo with two smaller scenes at right. Captions sit below images. Mobile reads as a single column without sticky positioning. Images open at full size.

FORM: Narrow extension of the established surface; no new-world seed applies. Design variance 7, motion 3, density 4. Hover zoom acknowledges the full-image link; no automatic animation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
